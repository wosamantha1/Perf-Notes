A Note from the Creator

Throughout high school, I spent a lot of my time taking copious, detailed notes in each of my classes. When it came time to prepare for major exams, my notes made my studying so much easier. Oftentimes, I would send my notes to friends and students I tutor, but I wanted to make a larger impact.

I decided to create this website to share my notes and other helpful materials with students everywhere. As someone who deeply values education and helping others, I hope Perf Notes will become a helpful resource for you, making studying a little easier and less overwhelming.

My ultimate goal is to help you achieve academic success, and I hope Perf Notes can help you get there.

You can do it!
— Samantha Wo
